Sruti Trainer — Support

Sruti Trainer helps you sing Carnatic swaras in tune, with a live pitch line, a tanpura on your Sa and simple exercises. It's free, with no account.

Getting started

  1. Set your Sa with the Sa button at the top-left (or try Find my Sa).
  2. Tap Start and allow the microphone.
  3. Sing or hum. Keep the coloured line inside the green band — the app tells you to sing higher or lower.

Common questions

The line stops or jumps when the tanpura plays. Use headphones or AirPods so the microphone hears only you. Playing the tanpura from the phone speaker is experimental.

Does the tanpura follow the note I sing? No — it stays on Sa (and Pa) on purpose, as your reference. Turn on Guide to also hear the note you're aiming for.

What do the dots on the swaras mean? A dot above is the upper octave (Ṡa), a dot below is the lower octave (Ṇi).

Something looks wrong. Open Settings ▸ Diagnostics ▸ “Share the last 30 seconds” and email it with a short description — it shows exactly what the microphone heard.
