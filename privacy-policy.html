Sruti Trainer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Sruti Trainer is a free singing pitch trainer. It is designed to work entirely on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Sruti Trainer does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only to measure the pitch of your voice while you practise. The sound is analysed live on your device and is not recorded or sent anywhere.

An optional diagnostic setting, “Record each session”, saves microphone recordings in the app’s own folder on your phone. It is off by default. These files never leave your phone unless you choose to share one yourself (for example with the Share button), and they are deleted if you delete the app.

Data on your device

Your settings (such as your Sa), the exercises you create and your practice history are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
